# MiniNAS for Android — Official Releases

**MiniNAS** turns your Android phone or tablet into a LAN NAS (network-attached storage): share folders over WebDAV / FTP on your local network — computers, TVs and other devices connect directly. Works fully offline; zero data upload.

MiniNAS 把你的 Android 手机/平板变成一台局域网 NAS：通过 WebDAV / FTP 向局域网共享文件夹，电脑、电视直接连接。完全离线工作，零数据上传。

## Download 下载

Go to [**Releases**](https://github.com/Roggewei/mininas-android-releases/releases) and grab the latest APK. Requires **Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later**.

前往 [**Releases**](https://github.com/Roggewei/mininas-android-releases/releases) 下载最新 APK。需要 **Android 8.0 及以上**。安装包约 4.6MB。
