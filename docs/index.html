MiniNAS 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

MiniNAS Android 版（以下简称"本软件"）坚持"数据不出本机"的设计原则。请花一分钟了解本软件如何处理数据。

一、我们不收集任何数据

  1. 零收集、零上传：本软件不包含任何统计、遥测、广告或崩溃上报组件，不会将您的任何信息发送到发布者或第三方的服务器。
  2. 无账号体系：本软件不要求注册，不与任何在线账号绑定。

二、本机存储的数据

以下数据仅保存在您设备的应用私有目录中（其他应用无法读取），供软件运行使用：

数据 存储位置 用途
共享文件夹配置、账号密码、外观与语言设置 应用私有目录 config.json 记住您的设置
活动日志 应用私有目录 logs/ 供您查看共享活动记录

您可以随时卸载应用（配置与日志一并清除），或在系统文件管理器中查看、删除接收目录中的文件。

三、局域网通信

  1. 使用共享功能时，您的设备会在局域网内与其他设备直接通信（TCP/UDP、mDNS 组播）；共享文件的传输路径为局域网内设备之间，不经过任何外部服务器。
  2. 为在局域网内被发现，本软件会广播设备名称与所提供的服务类型（类似"打印机发现"机制），这些信息仅在同一局域网内可见。

四、系统权限的使用

  1. 所有文件访问权限：用于读取您选择共享的文件夹内容并向局域网提供共享服务。这是本软件核心功能的必要权限；您未共享的文件夹不会被主动访问。
  2. 通知权限：用于在前台通知栏常驻服务状态通知（可关闭通知，不影响共享服务本身）。
  3. 本软件不申请定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风等任何其他敏感权限。

五、未成年人保护

本软件不面向未成年人收集任何信息（本软件对任何人都不收集信息）。

六、政策变更

如本政策发生变更，将在新版本软件首次启动时提示您查看。本政策以中文版本为准。

七、联系渠道

对本政策有任何疑问，可通过本软件发布渠道（应用商店页面 / 项目仓库）与我们联系。

MiniNAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

MiniNAS for Android (the "Software") is built on the principle that data never leaves your device. Please take a minute to see how the Software handles data.

1. We Collect Nothing

  1. Zero collection, zero upload: the Software contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting components, and never sends any of your information to the publisher's or any third party's servers.
  2. No account system: the Software requires no registration and is not tied to any online account.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only in the app's private directory on your device (not readable by other apps) and is used solely to run the Software:

Data Location Purpose
Share configuration, account credentials, appearance & language settings App-private config.json Remembering your settings
Activity logs App-private logs/ Showing you sharing activity

You can uninstall the app at any time (configuration and logs are removed with it), and view or delete files in the receive folder from your file manager.

3. Local Network Communication

  1. When sharing, your device communicates within the LAN directly with other devices (TCP/UDP, mDNS multicast); files travel between devices on your local network and never through any external server.
  2. To be discovered on the LAN, the Software broadcasts your device name and the services it offers (similar to "printer discovery"); this information is visible only on the same local network.

4. System Permissions

  1. All files access: used to read the contents of folders you choose to share and serve them on the LAN. It is required for the Software's core function; folders you have not shared are not actively accessed.
  2. Notifications: used for the persistent foreground-service status notification (you may disable the notification; the sharing service itself is unaffected).
  3. The Software requests no other sensitive permissions (no location, contacts, camera or microphone).

5. Minors

The Software collects no information from minors (it collects no information from anyone).

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, new versions of the Software will ask you to review it on first launch.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, reach us through the Software's distribution channel (app store page / project repository).

Note: the Chinese version of this policy is the authoritative version; this English translation is provided for reference only.
